# Rodent Retail
Repository for game jam

Scavenge urban trash, craft miniature furniture and decor, and (one day) sell your creations to the undercity’s tiny residents.

This is a short demo created for the GameDev.tv Game Jam 2025: Tiny World.
In this slice of gameplay, you’ll experience one day in the life of a resourceful rat — scouring the city for junk, combining materials, and crafting tiny treasures.

Play here https://malkaviana.itch.io/rodent-retail
